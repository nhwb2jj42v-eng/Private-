Information is used only to operate, maintain, improve, and provide the bot's features and to respond to user requests.

We do not sell or publicly disclose users' personal information. Information may be processed by services necessary for the operation of the bot.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's operation or for legitimate technical and security purposes.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. User Requests

If you want information associated with your use of the bot to be deleted, contact the bot administrator through the contact method provided by the bot.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the administrator of this Telegram bot.
